# Product: Frankenstudio
One idea in, one finished, publishable piece out.
A solo creator types an idea (or pastes a link). Frankenstudio stitches parts that were never built to work together — a planner LLM, a writer, a code-drawn illustrator, a headless browser researcher, ffmpeg and a critic — into one pipeline, and hands back a long-image article plus a 30-second vertical video.
Audience: solo AI/tech creators who publish on X, 小红书, 公众号 and have no team.
Category: Kiroween 2026 · Frankenstein.
Non-goals: auto-posting to any social account (the user always presses publish), scraping behind logins, crypto.
